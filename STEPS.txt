RILEY RAG — what's new
• New-issue alerts: when an editor publishes an issue, every member who opted in gets a phone notification.
• A friendly "Keep the Rag on your phone" guide pops up for visitors on phones (once a week at most), with
  picture-steps for iPhone, a one-tap button on Android, and a "copy link → open in Safari" fix for people who
  opened the link inside Facebook, Gmail, etc. There's also a "Put the Rag on your phone" button on the sign-in page,
  and an "On your phone" section on the Us page (add to home screen, alerts on/off).
• Fixed: the Rag's offline helper (sw.js) never actually started; it does now, and new uploads always show up fresh.
• Brown-and-gold icons (the repo still had the green Rangers ones).

2. Supabase → SQL Editor → paste rr_push.sql → Run.
3. Supabase → Edge Functions → Deploy a new function → Via Editor → name it  rr-notify  → paste rr-notify-index.ts → Deploy.
   Leave "Verify JWT" ON for this one (only signed-in editors can trigger it).
